Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. We Do Not Collect Your Data

All tools on Bedtime Cycle run entirely in your web browser. Your sleep data, bedtime calculations, and personal inputs are stored in your browser's localStorage only. We do not have a database. We do not have a server that processes your inputs. We literally cannot see what you enter.

2. No Cookies for Tracking

We do not use cookies to track your behavior across the web. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. The only cookies that may exist are essential for basic site functionality.

3. Advertising

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. These ads may use cookies to serve relevant content. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

4. Affiliate Links

Some articles contain affiliate links to sleep products and mattresses. If you click and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed in our Affiliate Disclosure.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email privacy@bedtimecycle.com.
